Our Story

Where Two Worlds Meet on One Crust

Nestled in the heart of Powell, OH — one of Columbus's most vibrant neighborhoods — Pizzatwist brings you a dining experience you won't find anywhere else. We blend the time-honored traditions of Italian pizza-making with the bold, aromatic spices of Indian cuisine, creating flavors that surprise and delight with every bite.

Whether you're craving a classic Margherita or an adventurous Tikka Masala pizza, our hand-stretched Italian crust is the perfect canvas. Every pie is crafted fresh to order using locally sourced ingredients and imported Indian spices — authentic taste from two beloved culinary traditions.

Stop by our Powell location at 258 W Olentangy St, call ahead for carry-out, or order online in just a few clicks. We're proud to serve the Powell community and the greater Columbus area.
